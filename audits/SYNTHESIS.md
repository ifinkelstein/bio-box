## 3. Master List of All 25 Architectural Upgrades Applied to `PLAN.md` v2.0

### A. Biological Fidelity Upgrades (`BIO-01` .. `BIO-11`)
1. **[`BIO-01`] Polymorphic Toxin Tip (`CTD`) Guard & Slicing:** `MMseqs2` `NR90`/`NR50` clustering enforces a **terminal 120-aa sliding-window identity check ($\ge 90\%$)** so `Rhs`/`CdiA`/`VgrG`/`LXG`/`MafB` proteins with distinct C-terminal toxin tips are never collapsed. Proteins carrying N-terminal delivery scaffolds + `pre-tox` motifs (`PT-HINT`, `VENV`, `DPTG`, `cinA`) have their downstream `CTD` (`50–180 aa`) sliced into an explicit `CTD_TOXIN_TIP` segment for isolated `hhsearch` and `ProstT5`/`Foldseek`.
2. **[`BIO-02`] Discontinuous / Nested ("Russian-Doll") Domain Stitching:** `cath-resolve-hits` is upgraded to allow discontinuous HMM envelope segments (`gap >= 35 aa` occupied by an insert domain) and records `parent_seg_idx_in_orf: int8` and `is_discontinuous_host: bool`. Active-site bitmasks (`catalytic_motif_hash`, `catalytic_intact`) are evaluated on the **reassembled parent domain alignment** (preserving split `RuvC` in `Cas9` and split `AspRS` catalytic cores).
3. **[`BIO-03`] Circular Permutation (`CP`) Detection:** `pyhmmer` and `Foldseek` (`--alignment-type 1` / local TM-align) detect swapped N-to-C fragment orders (`N-frag -> C-frag` vs. model `C-frag -> N-frag`, as in Class I SAM MTases, `PLD` nucleases, swiveling $\beta/\beta/\alpha$ barrels, and `Lit`/`Rex` phages), stitching them with `is_circularly_permuted: bool = true` before catalytic checking.
4. **[`BIO-04`] Homotypic Solenoid & Tandem Repeat Collapse:** Consecutive same-clan repeat units (`TPR`, `ANK`, `LRR`, `WD40`, `PPR`, `HEAT`, `Pentapeptide`, `RHS_repeat`, `SLH`, `CW_binding`) within $\le 15\text{ aa}$ are collapsed into a **single super-segment** (`is_repeat_array = true`, `repeat_unit_count: uint8`), preventing repeat arrays from flooding `slot_idx` and `gLM-Domain`'s 64-slot window.
5. **[`BIO-05`] 5-Mode Phylum-Aware Translation Initiation & `smORF` Calling:** Replaces the *E. coli*-biased `AGGAG`/`GGAGG` requirement with a 5-mode caller supporting canonical SD, translational coupling (`-4`/`-1` overlaps), **leaderless archaeal/actinobacterial transcripts** (`TATA`/`BRE` or `-10 TANNNT` within `0–6 bp` of `5'-ATG/GTG`), ***Bacteroidota* AT-rich initiation windows** (`rpsA`/`bS1` A/U enhancer), and RBS-independent `pyhmmer`/`TM`/`RNAcode` rescue.
6. **[`BIO-06`] Stage 1.4 Coordinate Overlap Arbitrator:** Purges false-positive `pyrodigal`/`smORF` calls shadowed inside `Minced` CRISPR arrays, `rRNAs`, `tRNAs`, or structured riboswitch cores (unless backed by a verified protein HMM hit $E < 10^{-5}$), while preserving and linking legitimate sense `uORF_cis_leader` attenuators, `CreTA` CRISPR addiction toxins, and antisense Type I TA modules (`hok/sok`, `tisB/istR`).
7. **[`BIO-07`] Stage 1.5 Split-Gene, Programmed Frameshift, `Sec`/`Pyl`, & Intron Reconstructor:** Stitches adjacent same-strand ORFs matching continuous reference profiles across programmed `-1`/`+1` frameshifts (`prfB`, phage `gpG-gpGT`, `IS1`/`IS3` `OrfAB`), `UGA` selenocysteine (`bSECIS` + `selA/B/C/D`), `UAG` pyrrolysine (`pylT/S/B/C/D`), and phage/archaeal Group I/II introns (`nrdA`, `td`, `polA`, `terL`) with embedded homing endonucleases (`LAGLIDADG`, `GIY-YIG`, `HNH`), plus an **Obelisk (`700–1,500 bp` circular RNA + Hammerhead ribozyme)** whitelist in the Stage 0 sieve.
8. **[`BIO-08` & `BIO-11`] Sub-Family & Riboswitch Binding-Pocket Subtyping (`subfamily_50_id` + `catalytic_motif_hash`):** Splits large superfamilies (`PF04055`, `PF00005`, `PF00534`) by 50% domain-envelope identity + active-site pocket signature (`catalytic_motif_hash`), and sub-types `Infernal` riboswitch hits using covariance pocket-column rules (`cm_pocket_rules.json` separating `ykkC` `Guanidine-I`, `ppGpp`, `PRPP`, `NDP`; purine `G`/`A`/`2'-dG`; `c-di-GMP` vs `c-AMP-GMP`).
9. **[`BIO-09`] 3-Plane Pangenome Bitvectors & Contig-Edge Veto:** Replaces single-genome species-rep bitvectors with `core_chr_bits`, `pan_any_bits`, and `mge_accessory_bits` (`14,144 bytes` each) gated by `hq_closed_or_multi_strain_mask` and `operon_not_at_contig_edge` so MAG fragmentation and accessory defense-island sparsity never fabricate false NOGD holes.
10. **[`BIO-10`] Non-Collinear Regulon Topologies (`regulon_locus_id` & `junction_topology_prev`) + Attenuator Exemption:** Exempts `5'-cis-attenuator` terminators (`feature_kind = 9`) from breaking `directon_id`; measures `delta_bp_prev_slot` from the preceding RNA or CDS slot; and adds `regulon_locus_id: uint32` and 6-state `junction_topology_prev` (`INTRA_ORF_FUSION`, `CO_DIRECTIONAL`, `DIVERGON_PROMOTER`, `CONVERGON_EXCLUDON`, `SENSE_ANTISENSE_NESTED`, `INTER_LOCUS_BREAK`).
