## M5. Short contigs: the producer on a stride sample of phage contigs

**Sample.** Stage S1 of `/tmp/if2453/r10/root` (release `rN.3`): 27,258 contigs in 27,258 genomes, all `kind`
virus, none circular, 1,664,109,938 bases, median contig 45,229 bases (*measured*, `ncrna_set.py sample`). Rule: the
stage's `genome_input` contigs sorted by `contig_uid`, lengths from its `contig_index` (default variant); k =
1,664,109,938 // 50,000,000 = 33; every contig whose 0-based rank i satisfies i % 33 == 0. That gives 826 contigs,
46,990,526 bases: contig length min 3,911, p10 5,232, median 43,947, p90 139,439, max 366,556; 68,022 gene calls
(`pyrodigal-3.7.1-gv-0.3.2-p2`, the stage's), median 64 per contig (*measured*). The sample was cut in two parts by
cumulative bases in the same order (408 contigs and 23,513,674 bases; 418 and 23,476,852), one process each.

**How the producer ran.** `bio-box stage ncrna` scans every contig of a stage's `genome_input` (its `plan` reads the
whole table), so it cannot scan a sample; it accepts `--out`, but under `run-step` that is the staging directory
that run-step publishes into the root. So each part called the producer's own function, `biobox.l1.ncrna.run` (the
one `stage ncrna` calls), with `genomes=` the part's contigs (one `GenomeInput` per contig, circular flag from
`genome_input`), `out=` a scratch root `/tmp/if2453/m08b/m5/out<k>`, `tmp=` scratch, `cpus=2`, the set `prok-1`
(565 intergenic models at margin 200 with `--notrunc`, 23 in-CDS models on whole contigs) and clan competition. It
read stage S1's sequences and gene calls from the release root, which was bound read-only into the container
(nothing was written there; a 50 Gbp build was writing other stages of that root at the time). Producer name
`infernal-1.1.5-rfam-15.1-prok1-igr200nt-clan1`, as card E-08's. To see each `cmsearch` call, a wrapper first on the
container's `PATH` (`cmsearch_wrap.sh`) ran the binary under `timed.py` with the producer's argv, except that `-o
/dev/null` became `-o <file>` so each model's own `# CPU time` line was kept (as 0-09c did).

## Reading (my interpretation)

On complete phage genomes the producer costs less per base than on the 8 genomes (165 against 255 to 261 CPU-hours per
10 Gbp), and the reason is content, not records: phage contigs give the same number of intergenic records per base at
a 200-base margin, and without the rRNA models (a quarter of the intergenic cost where operons exist, 2% on phages) the
cost per base is within 6%. So cutting shards by bases at the slower prokaryote rate (304.0) is safe for phage stages
and wastes headroom there (a phage shard would use about 130 of 300 CPU-minutes); the case that could break it is a
shard dense in rRNA operons, which E-08's 314.9 already shows, and fragmented metagenome contigs, which S1 does not
contain and this card did not measure. For the tRNA choice in DEC-28: Infernal's filters, at the fixed Z, cost real
genomes few tRNAs, none in four of the six genomes scanned and one borderline locus in E. coli, far from the panel's 2
in 5; the loss concentrates in the jumbo phage LAK-A1 (5 of 37 loci, three of them strong hits, four of them longer
than a canonical tRNA), and the filtered pipeline also cuts three tRNAs short, two of them 91-base archaeal tRNAs. That
is a case for a dedicated tRNA caller's annotation (intron and isotype calls) on phage and archaeal tRNAs rather than
for turning the filters off: `--max` costs about 10,000 times the default per base, about 7,900 CPU-hours per 10 Gbp
for RF00005 alone (*derived*: 2.84 CPU-ms × 1e10 / 3,600). Whether tRNAscan-SE finds these loci is M3, which needs
the PI's environment change.

## What was not measured, and why

- M2 on B. subtilis and M. tuberculosis, and tRNA-Sec (RF01852) with `--max` on any genome but lambda and T4: the
  cost of `--max` measured 2.3 times the design note's estimate for the whole of M2, so the scan was cut to what fits
  in that estimate (AGENTS §6, §9); finishing it costs about 6.8 CPU-hours for RF00005 and 8.8 for RF01852
  (*derived*, section M2).
- Published tRNA gene counts for these genomes: not on the pod, and nothing was fetched (AGENTS §8); M2 compares the
  two scans with each other only.
- M2 on the producer's own records (intergenic, margin 200, `--notrunc`): not run; every locus found only with
  `--max` lies wholly inside those regions, and the default filters act per window, so I expect the producer to miss
  the same loci (*my reading*, not measured).
- M5 on metagenome-like fragments: stage S1 holds complete phage genomes (shortest in the sample 3,911 bases, median
  43,947), so the record count per base of short fragments, and the in-CDS models' truncated-hit search at many
  contig ends, are not measured. No such stage is in the release root.
- M5 through `bio-box stage ncrna` itself: the command has no genome subset (section M5); the same function ran with a
  sample.
- M4: nothing left (section M4).
