## Every prok-1 feature against prok-2

- **Models whose flag is the same in both sets** (584 of prok-1's 588): all 1,024 prok-1 rows of these models are
  prok-2 rows, with the same coordinates, bit score, E-value, model span, truncation flags and GC. *Measured*.
- **Clan competition** takes no prok-1 feature: no prok-1 winner loses in prok-2, and the 52 losers are the same hits.
- **The 4 models DEC-30 moved to whole genome** (RF01670 P17, RF02111 IS009, RF02820 V_AS9, RF02908 rli30): only
  IS009 hits these genomes. RF01670, RF02820 and RF02908 have no hit with either set. Every prok-1 hit of the four, and
  what became of it (*measured*, `e09_diff.py`, E. coli MG1655 unless stated):

| IS009 303,610-303,810 (+), 202.3 | the same row (coordinates and every value) | same |

  coordinates, plus 2 new IS009 loci. No feature disappeared.

## Reading

The producer with `prok-2` reproduces the configuration's truth exactly (1,029 of 1,029, nothing else). The cost is
314.1 CPU-hours per 10 Gbp, 4.8% above DEC-30's derived 299.7. Against prok-1 on the same stage, on the same host,
the measured ratio is 1.20 to 1.23 (two prok-1 runs), against the derived 1.178. On these 8 genomes the 182 added
models find nothing, as expected: the sample that chose them did not include these genomes, and the long tail is
lineage-specific.

The derivation already counted the 4 switched models at their whole-genome CPU lines, so they do not explain the gap.
The cause of the higher measured ratio is not established here. Candidates are fixed per-call costs of the larger
CM files, and the host's variation between runs (2.6% between cards E-07 and E-08 on the same code). Shard 1 (the *E. coli*,
*B. subtilis* and one more contig, as in cards E-07 and E-08) ran 2.2% above the 358.0 that the planner now cuts
prok-2 shards at. A shard cut at 80% of the budget would still use only about 245 of 300 CPU-minutes.

Of the four flag changes, only IS009 matters here. Its two prok-1 hits that ended at a region boundary are now full
length, and two loci inside genes are new; no prok-1 hit is lost.

## Commands

On the laptop: `sh scripts/pod_pytest.sh E-09 ...` mirrored the tree to `/tmp/if2453/ci/E-09`. On the pod it was
copied to `/tmp/if2453/e09/tree` (`rsync -a --delete`), `/tmp/if2453/m09c/root` was copied to `/tmp/if2453/e09/root`
(`cp -a`), and the profile `/tmp/if2453/e09/e09-profile.yaml` is card E-08's with its paths under
`/tmp/if2453/e09`.

The driver `/tmp/if2453/e09/e09_driver.sh` ran in unit `e09-driver` (`MemoryMax=4G`, `CPUQuota=100%`, nice 19).
- Before each launch it summed `ps -u if2453 -o pcpu=` and waited until that plus the launch's threads fit the
  account's cap (5600% at night).
- It also waited while `/tmp/if2453/r10/root/STOP` (the 50 Gbp build's) existed: 20:23 to 20:35.
- Each step ran in its own `pod_run.sh` unit (`MemoryMax=16G`, `MemorySwapMax=0`, nice 19), the two shards at once
  with two threads each:

```bash
P=infernal-1.1.5-rfam-15.1-prok2-igr200nt-clan1
RUN="bash /tmp/if2453/e09/tree/scripts/pod_run.sh --profile /tmp/if2453/e09/e09-profile.yaml --image /stor/home/if2453/bio-box/images/bio-box-test_74b3e05.sif --bind /tmp/if2453 --env PYTHONPATH=/tmp/if2453/e09/tree/src:/tmp/if2453/pilot_s1/pydeps --mem 16G"
STEP="run-step --profile /tmp/if2453/e09/e09-profile.yaml --root /tmp/if2453/e09/root --stage M9C"
NC="python -m biobox.cli stage ncrna"
NCA="--root /tmp/if2453/e09/root --stage M9C --cm /tmp/if2453/golden/rfam/Rfam.cm.gz --ncrna-set prok-2"
$RUN --threads 1 e09-plan $STEP --step ncrna-$P.plan --threads 1 -- $NC plan $NCA --scan-bases 9500000 --tmp /tmp/if2453/e09/tmp/plan
$RUN --threads 2 e09-shard-0 $STEP --step ncrna-$P.shard-00000 --threads 2 -- $NC shard $NCA --shard 0 --cpus 2 --tmp /tmp/if2453/e09/tmp/scan --seq-stages M9C
$RUN --threads 2 e09-shard-1 $STEP --step ncrna-$P.shard-00001 --threads 2 -- $NC shard $NCA --shard 1 --cpus 2 --tmp /tmp/if2453/e09/tmp/scan --seq-stages M9C
$RUN --threads 1 e09-merge $STEP --step ncrna-$P --threads 1 -- $NC merge $NCA --tmp /tmp/if2453/e09/tmp/scan
```
