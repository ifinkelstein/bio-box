## 11. The drain-stop (what it is)

`drain_stop` in `c26_driver.sh` is the rate guard's action for a gene-call batch more than 20% off its estimate
(`c26_est.py rates` exits 4 when every judged miss is a batch). The four batches run at once and end together, so
killing the unit on the first batch's record would throw away about three batches' work (about 650 CPU-minutes,
*derived*). Instead the driver waits until every batch is recorded (or none runs in the unit), then writes its
token-marked `STOP`, which ends the launch before the merge at a step boundary (RUNBOOK §3), removes it, and fails: no
batch's work is lost and the miss is still reported. It was written on 2026-11-15 during the rehearsal, when the
rehearsal's batches came in at 0.794 to 0.797 of their (tiny) estimates; it was in the driver for the rehearsal, the
before mode and both launches (the copy on the pod dated 20:44 is the committed `ef05cc9` version), and it was never
exercised: the real batches ran at 0.961 to 0.980.
