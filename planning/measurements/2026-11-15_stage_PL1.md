- A resume is the same command (`c26_driver.sh run`, unit `c26-pl1-2`): `inputs` and `ingest` are skipped, gene calling
  starts from its plan. With the guard as it is, the next judged steps are the 4 gene-call batches (218.3 each). The
  batch estimate carries `STEP_OVERHEAD` 1.23, the largest record-to-calls ratio of the built stages; S1's batches ran
  at 0.85 to 0.88 of theirs (card B-48) and the rehearsal's tiny batches at 0.794 to 0.797 (*measured*, not judged).
  So a batch near or under 0.80 is possible (*estimate*); the scan's full shards ran at 0.887 to 0.920 on H1.
- For that case the driver does not kill the unit (the four batches run at once and end together, about 650
  CPU-minutes of the others' work would be thrown away): `drain_stop` lets the other batches publish, then writes its
  `STOP`, which ends the launch before the merge (a step boundary, RUNBOOK §3), and fails. It was written after the
  rehearsal showed batches near 0.80; it is in the driver that ran (`ef05cc9`) but was never exercised.
