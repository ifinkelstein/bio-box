## 3. Findings that bear on the cards that use the index

1. **GlobDB identifiers are stable across r226 and r232 for every genome both releases hold** (*measured*): of the 70,314 unbuilt r226 genomes that r232 keeps, all 70,314 keep their identifier (`same_original_id` 68,975, no dictionary and same id 1,339). But the dictionaries must still be read through the catalogue's own name: r232 writes the RBG originals with a `.fna` suffix and the ids with `.fa` (`binchicken_co100_111.fna`, `BCRBG_00001.fa`), and a naive comparison of r226 and r232 dictionaries reports 38,494 differing rows that are only this suffix (*measured*, an exploration on the same files). The index strips `.fna`, `.fa` and `.fasta`.
2. **No INSDC accession for most resources.** Only some dictionaries carry `GCA_`/`GCF_`: lines holding one are AMXMAG 80, GEM 19,516, GOMC 4,271 and SPIRE 14,944 (`grep -c`, 2026-11-26); mOTU, RBG, GCMETA, SMAG, TPMC and the rest give names of the source catalogue, not INSDC accessions. **A "not in GTDB R232 / GlobDB r232 by accession" test therefore cannot see a genome of those resources.** Cards C-75 to C-81 use the accession test for what it can say (an INSDC-named genome) and the ANI tests of their own cards for the rest, as the plan says (§0 (5)).
3. **r232 holds fewer of the r226 non-GTDB genomes than a re-key by identifier suggests**: see §4. `GFS` (1,377 genomes) is not a resource of r232 at all.

## 4. The re-key of the 130,960 unbuilt r226 genomes

The 130,960 are the r226 non-GTDB genomes of `/tmp/if2453/s3/globdb/tables/non_gtdb_stages.tsv` (162,653 rows) that are neither in `G1` nor in a phylum of `GART1`/`GART2` (`r232_index.py unbuilt`; 155,514 - 24,554 = 130,960 as the C-63 note derives, *measured* equal). `rekey` returns, for each: the same genome's r232 identifier (`kept`), the r232 genome that now stands for it (`replaced`), or `dropped` with a reason; an identifier r226 never had is `unknown`. Rules, in order: a resource r232 does not have is `dropped`; the genome's original identifier (its name in the source catalogue, from the r226 dictionary) found in r232's dictionary under an identifier that is in r232's taxonomy is `kept`; an original that is an INSDC accession now in GTDB R232 is `replaced` by the GlobDB r232 identifier of that accession's GTDB representative; a cluster id (SPIRE, GEM) that r232 gives to another genome is `replaced` by it; the rest is `dropped`. The ids are never compared alone (test: an identifier reused for another genome is not `kept`). Output `/tmp/if2453/c74/rekey_unbuilt.tsv` (sha256 `780225326633577e7e87a24e9f11c029184a8b2a1d84350f1a11a72968f8988f`, columns `r226_id, status, r232_id, reason`) from `/tmp/if2453/c74/unbuilt_ids.txt` (`33a46cf0692d032d998181521d576da027df75d3dfec6ef4c5e80d1a3cab5d0c`); the same for all 162,653 non-GTDB genomes in `rekey_all_non_gtdb.tsv` (`1a505f529c26c6684034201e9ceb5746d47eeb12ef80e3137ad153dc9a89e629`). "Dropped, dictionary lists it" means r232's dereplication left the genome out of GlobDB r232 (a species represented by another genome or by a GTDB R232 representative); the tables do not say which genome replaced it, and this card does not guess: a species-level test needs the sequences (ANI), which DEC-79 (2) postpones to `r26.11.6`. For card C-75 the rule that follows from DEC-79 (3) is the card's own: a `dropped` genome is still taken from r226 when it passes the selection, with its `rekey` row beside it. The 984 `GFS` genomes are among the 130,960 and are in no r232 table. Only an INSDC-named genome can be `replaced` by this method (73 here); no `same_cluster_id` case occurred (*measured*).
