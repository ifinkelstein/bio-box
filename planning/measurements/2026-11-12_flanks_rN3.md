## The pfam level fragments phage loci

At pfam the same groups split into far more types: 2,119 against 598 at nr50; the 214-locus group is 214 types (5 at
nr50), the 122-locus group 120 (2). Phage flanks hold many genes without a Pfam domain, and each is a token of its own
(the card's rule: an unannotated neighbour never makes two loci similar), so two loci with, say, five unannotated
genes each share at most 5 of 15 tokens and J ≥ 0.67, above the cut of 0.5 (*derived* from the definition). nr50 is
the level to use on phages; the default is nr50. Whether pfam should compare annotated positions only is a question
for the PI (report of card W-25).
