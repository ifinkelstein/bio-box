## 2. Deterministic selection rule and `MV1` composition

`c62_metavr_inputs.py` excludes all `is_concatemer == True` UViGs, selects one best non-concatemer representative per `votu` by `(quality_rank, -min(completeness, 100.0), contamination, -length, uvig)`, prioritizes five tiers, and takes the longest strict prefix within `3,000,000` contigs and `40,000,000,000` bp (`mv1_report.json`, *measured*):

| Priority tier | Rule | Selected UViGs | Selected bp |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1. `jumbo_ge200k` | All non-concatemer `>= 200 kb` (reps + non-reps) | `16,379` | `4,978,613,520` |
| 2. `rep_complete_hq` | Complete & High-quality vOTU reps (`< 200 kb`, contam `<= 10%`) | `374,317` | `13,211,462,966` |
| 3. `rep_mq_ge30k` | Medium-quality vOTU reps (`30–200 kb`, contam `<= 10%`) | `191,729` | `9,461,563,384` |
| 4. `rep_mq_lt30k` | Medium-quality vOTU reps (`< 30 kb`, contam `<= 10%`) | `287,204` | `4,455,542,825` |
| 5. `rep_large_ge30k_lq_nd` | Top prefix of `>= 30 kb` LQ/ND vOTU reps (High conf first, longest first) | `146,975` | `7,892,788,942` |
| **Total `MV1` selected** | | **`1,016,604`** | **`39,999,971,637`** |

Upstream metadata discrepancy (*measured*): `10` multi-contig `Virus MAG` (`GVMAG-M-*`) UViGs had `sum(FASTA contig lengths)` smaller than `METAVR_main_table.parquet`'s `length` column (total `4,478,752` vs `6,467,274` bp; `METAVR.fna.bgz` is `2026-08-26` vs `METAVR_main_table.parquet` `2026-06-24`). All `10` are recorded in `mv1_report.json` (`length_mismatches`) and staged with their actual FASTA bytes and lengths (`0` missing UViGs across `24,635,130` scanned records).
