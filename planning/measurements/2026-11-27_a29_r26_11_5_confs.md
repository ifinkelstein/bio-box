tuple `R26_11_5_CHAIN` of the script, which the ownership run, `write-confs` and the tests read. **The chain ends at

a first comment line saying so, the chain's `AFTER=`, this note as a `NOTE`, and the comment corrections below; every
other line is the template's (its `SUM`, `INPUTS`, depth, library, mode, `CRISPR`, `JUDGE` and `EXPECT` lines). The
templates themselves are not changed (they are not this card's files, and the staging cards' tests read them).
