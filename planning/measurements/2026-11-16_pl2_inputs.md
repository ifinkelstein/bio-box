### Topology, completeness and terminal repeats

- **Mapping rules (`c40_spec.py`, `make_inputs.py plasmid-sources`)**:
  - `gut_plasmids_yu2024`: `circular` `True` → topology `circular`, completeness `complete` (`assembly_quality =
    complete`, 4,517 records); `False` → topology `linear`, completeness `unknown` (`assembly_quality = contig`, 63,833
    records). No MobMess `fragment` cluster has a representative (`cluster_type = fragment` has 0 rows with
    `in_dereplicated_plasmids = True`, *measured*, `summary.json`).
  - PlasmidScope: `Topology` `circular` → `circular`; `linear`, `direct terminal repeat`, `inverted terminal repeat` and
    `concatemer` → `linear`. `Completeness` `complete` → `complete` (`assembly_quality = complete`); `incomplete` →
    `fragment` (`assembly_quality = contig`); `-` → `unknown` (`assembly_quality = contig`).
  - **Fragments are never circular**: a record whose table marks it `incomplete` (`fragment`) is forced linear even when
    its `Topology` column says `circular`. That rule overrode **134 taken records**: `GenBank` 129, `ENA` 4, `DDBJ` 1
    (`circular_but_fragment`, *measured*, `report.json`).
- **Terminal-repeat check on `IMG-PR` DTR contigs and circular gut plasmids (`c40_analyze.py`)**:
  - `IMG-PR` never uses `Topology = circular` (`linear` 548,513, `direct terminal repeat` 142,805, `concatemer` 5,441,
    `inverted terminal repeat` 3,212; *measured*, `report.json`), so 0 `IMG-PR` genomes are circular in `PL2`. Searching
    each of the **142,805 DTR records** for an exact match of ≥20 bp from the contig's start inside its last 5,000 bp
    (`terminal_repeat`, `summary.json` `terminal_repeats`) found **64,179 records (44.9%, *derived*) that still carry
    both copies of the terminal repeat** (`20-99` bp: 29,918; `100-999` bp: 34,078; `>=1000` bp: 183, *measured*) and
    **78,626 (55.1%) with none ≥20 bp** (*measured*). Wrapping an untrimmed DTR contig in circular mode (`genecall._genes`)
    would join two copies of the repeat across the origin; keeping all DTR records linear avoids duplicating genes in
    those 64,179 contigs while splitting at most one origin-spanning gene in trimmed circular ones.
  - On the **4,517 circular gut representatives**, the same check found **3,497 (77.4%, *derived*) carrying a 20–99 bp
    exact terminal repeat** (untrimmed assembly k-mer overlap) and **1,020 with none ≥20 bp** (*measured*,
    `summary.json`). Wrapping a contig that retains a 20–99 bp overlap duplicates those 20–99 bp at the origin (§6).
- **The 15,135 circular non-representative gut contigs**: across all 226,194 PlasX predictions in
  `predicted_plasmids_summary.txt`, Yu et al. mark **19,652 contigs circular** (*published*, the article; *measured* in
  `summary.json` `gut_table_derep_type_circular`: `True|maximal|True` 3,507 + `True|backbone|True` 1,010 = **4,517** in
  the dereplicated set; `False|backbone|True` 11,390 + `False|maximal|True` 3,745 = **15,135** outside it; `fragment`
  has 125,475 linear and 0 circular). Taking `in_dereplicated_plasmids = True` follows the authors' MobMess
  dereplication across 1,782 gut metagenomes, which leaves out those 15,135 redundant circular contigs (many in clusters
  whose chosen representative is a longer linear contig or another circular copy; §6).
- **Records skipped as `no_row` and empty archive files**: 35,358 FASTA records in six PlasmidScope archives had no row
  in their repository's curated `.plasmid_list.download.tsv` and were skipped under `without_row = "skip"`: `GenBank`
  16,499 (of which 8,042 repeat a sequence a taken record already carries; 711.6 Mbp in all), `ENA` 16,348 (1,170
  repeating a taken sequence; 203.8 Mbp), `DDBJ` 2,489 (184; 44.4 Mbp), `RefSeq` 14, `Kraken2` 7, `TPA` 1 (*measured*,
  `report.json` and `awk` over `records.tsv` and `skipped_records.tsv`, 2026-11-17). In the other direction, 38 table
  rows had no non-empty FASTA record (`selected_rows_without_record`: `DDBJ` 15, `ENA` 12, `GenBank` 9, `IMG-PR` 2;
  *measured*, `report.json`).
- **Non-`ACGTN` IUPAC codes**: 1,151 of the 859,036 new contigs (`RefSeq` 691, `GenBank` 234, `IMG-PR` 203, `ENA` 17,
  `DDBJ` 6; `gut` and `mMGE` 0) hold 41,887 ambiguity bases (`K` 7,940, `M` 7,725, `W` 7,566, `Y` 7,510, `R` 7,040, `S`
