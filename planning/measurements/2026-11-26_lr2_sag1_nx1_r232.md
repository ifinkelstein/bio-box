## 2. What the accession test can see

A genome's INSDC accession is read from its genome id (`ehomd:GCA_000406805.1`) or, for a single cell, from the ENA assembly table of `PRJEB33281` by the SAG's name (`ena_PRJEB33281_assemblies.tsv`, `assembly_name` to `accession`; the C-58 table, 12,715 rows). Card C-74's `lookup` then says whether GTDB R232 or GlobDB r232 holds it. C-74 found that only AMXMAG, GEM, GOMC and SPIRE dictionaries of GlobDB r232 carry INSDC accessions (its note §3): an INSDC-named genome is seen in full by GTDB R232 and in part by GlobDB r232. No ANI test is made here (no r232 genome is on the pod).
