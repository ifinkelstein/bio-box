## Clustering (O-CLUSTER), golden set, 8 threads

- 33,442 distinct proteins -> 33,010 NR90 clusters -> 29,219 NR50 clusters (MMseqs2 18.8cc5c, `cluster --cluster-mode 2 --cov-mode 1 -c 0.8`, `--min-seq-id` 0.9 then 0.5 on the NR90 representatives, `mergeclusters`).
- `cluster_member_aln`: 33,442 alignment rows per level, so every protein has its member-to-representative alignment (C-11 complete).
- Wall time 40.1 s at 8 threads, dominated by fixed MMseqs2 overhead at this size; ingest 1.5 s and gene calling 37.1 s in the same run.
- R2 clustering tests (9) pass on the pod: every stored CIGAR re-walked against the sequences reproduces the stored spans, alignment length and identity; output is byte-identical at 1 and 4 threads (MR-16).
