## 2. Genetic-Code Compatibility Audit (`pyrodigal 3.7.1`, `genecall.py`, `orfs.py`, `pack.py`)

Auditing the pinned environment (`uv.lock`: `pyrodigal == 3.7.1`, `pyrodigal-gv == 0.3.2`) and `src/biobox/` uncovered two critical invariants for `ET1`:

1. **`biobox/search/orfs.py` & `pack build` (`_check_codes`)**:
   - `biobox/search/pack.py` (`_check_codes`, lines 458–468) calls `orfs.check_code(code)` on every `genetic_code` in `feature_protein` and aborts `pack build` with `ValueError` if any code is unsupported.
   - `pyrodigal 3.7.1` natively supports NCBI tables `1–6, 9–16, 21–33` (including `29` and `30`).
   - **However**, `biobox/search/orfs.py` (`supported_codes()`, lines 19–22 & 51–72) only supports `pyhmmer.easel.GeneticCode` tables **`1–6, 9–14, 16, 21–25`** plus explicit override **`15`**. **Tables `29` (*Mesodinium*) and `30` (*Peritrichia*) raise `ValueError` in `orfs.check_code()`!**
   - **Mitigation implemented in `c68_euk_transcriptomes_logan_rnap.py`**:
     - `resolve_taxon_genetic_code()` maps taxa to their exact biological NCBI nuclear table (`1` standard default + Heterotrichea/*Stentor*/*Blepharisma* exemption; `6` ciliate/dasyclad/hexamita; `10` euplotid; `29` *Mesodinium*; `30` *Peritrichia*) and **dynamically checks `orfs.check_code(bio_code)`**.
     - Under the default `--unsupported-code-policy defer`, samples requiring `29` or `30` are cleanly deferred to `ET1_left_for_later.tsv.gz` (`reason=transl_table_29_unsupported_by_orfs_py` / `transl_table_30_unsupported_by_orfs_py`, `needed_capability=orfs_py_explicit_tables_29_30`) so zero mistranslated residues enter `protein_seq` and `pack build` can **never** crash.
     - Under `--unsupported-code-policy fallback_table6`, `29` and `30` map to Table `6` (which shares the identical TGA-only stop codon set, keeping ORFs unbroken while substituting `Q` at `TAA`/`TAG`).
     - **Future 2-line `orfs.py` unlock**: Adding `29: (1, {"TAA": "Y", "TAG": "Y"})` and `30: (1, {"TAA": "E", "TAG": "E"})` to `_EXPLICIT` in `src/biobox/search/orfs.py` will automatically allow `c68` to stage `29` and `30` natively with zero changes to `c68_euk_transcriptomes_logan_rnap.py`.

2. **`biobox/l1/genecall.py` Single-Mode Routing (`SINGLE_MODE_MIN_BP = 100,000`)**:
   - `genecall.mode_of("prokaryote", bases, genecall_mode="default")` runs single-mode Pyrodigal trained with `translation_table=genome.genetic_code` **only when `bases >= 100,000` bp** and `genecall_mode == "default"`.
   - Therefore `stage_et1()` groups all kept `>= 1,500` nt cDNAs of each transcriptome sample into **one multi-contig `GenomeInput` per sample** (`kind="prokaryote"`, `assembly_quality="contig"`, `circular=[]`), and `ET1.conf` explicitly sets `GENECALL_MODE=default` and `CRISPR=no`.
