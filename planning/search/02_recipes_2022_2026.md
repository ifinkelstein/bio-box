Reading the counts:
- **Neighborhood work is the most common step, not iterative search.** Q-NBR appears in 25 papers. Q-ITER appears in 12.
- **Structure has caught up with profile-profile search.** Q-STRUCT appears in 21 papers, Q-REMOTE in 17, and in most papers both are used together to adjudicate borderline hits.
- **Q-MOTIF and Q-ARCH are almost never the primary question but recur as filters.** Catalytic-residue gates and domain trimming appear in 11 and 12 papers.
- **Many recent defense papers never run a homology search to find the system.** Q-SYS on precomputed tables (PADLOC DB, DefenseFinder RefSeq calls, CDD precomputed on 24,757 genomes) is how they start.
- **RNA motif discovery is rare.** Five papers; none used CMfinder or R-scape. Infernal was used only with existing CMs.

## 3. Common recipe patterns (as step sequences)

**P1. Koonin-group PSSM/PSI-BLAST census with context-based curation.** This is the house style in ESCRT 2024, CRISPR-Cas 2025, Karamycheva 2026, YprA 2026, Juk 2025, END 2026 and Yutin 2024.
1. Seed is a family, not one sequence: COG/arCOG members, known Cas profiles, or curated references, aligned with MUSCLE5. In CRISPR-Cas 2025 the aligner depends on cluster size: FAMSA for >500 sequences, muscle5 super5 for 101–500, muscle5 mpc for ≤100 (`lit/2025_Makarova...txt:141`). The alignment is split into sub-alignments, one PSSM each (`lit/2026_Bell...txt:91`).
2. PSI-BLAST against an in-house complete-genome set (24,757 genomes, GenBank Nov 2021; or 47,545 genomes, Nov 2023), with compositional statistics off, "five iterations or until convergence", inclusion 1e-3 to 1e-4 (`lit/2024_Makarova_Tobiasson...txt:109`; `lit/2026_Karamycheva...txt:202`).
3. Cluster hits with MMseqs2 at 0.5 and align representatives. Build a FastTree tree.
4. Pull the neighborhood. GenBank coordinates and strand for ±5, ±10 or ±50 genes, annotated by PSI-BLAST against CDD/Pfam PSSMs at E 0.001–0.01.
5. Remove false positives by tree position plus neighborhood. Repeat at 0.75 and 0.9 clustering (`lit/2026_Bell...txt:93`). False negatives are rescued by context: "few false negatives (JukB neighbors) were included" (`lit/2025_Yuping...txt:159`).
6. Borderline members go to HHpred (web), then AF2/AF3 plus DALI. Catalytic residues are checked on the model.
7. Extend into clustered NR for abundance or for rare families: three PSI-BLAST iterations (`lit/2025_Makarova...txt:147`).

**P2. Zhang-lab HMM census over a frozen contig corpus.** Tn7 2023, Fanzor 2023, TIGR 2025.
1. Public Pfam/COG HMMs, or a custom HMM from extracted domains, searched with hmmsearch (HMMER 3.4). Thresholds: `ga_cut`, `-E 1e-4 --domE 1000 -Z 69000000`, or bitscore > 15.
2. Search the custom "521,828,662 contigs ... 6,932,321,054,498 bp" corpus (GenBank, WGS, JGI, MG-RAST; `lit/2023_Faure...Tn7...txt:2046`). Fanzor adds translated stop-to-stop ORFs > 100 aa (`lit/2023_Jiang...txt:2462`).
3. Aggregate hits into loci by distance: 20 kb, with operon = "2 codirectional ORFs separated by less than 50 bp". Drop or truncate at contig edges.
4. Cluster in cascades with MMseqs2 (100%/30% coverage to drop partials, then 70/70, then 50/70; `lit/2025_Faure...TIGR...txt:181-185`).
5. Gate on alignment: catalytic motifs must be aligned (Walker A/B, CxxC, DED). Trim envelopes using AF2-defined cores.
6. Annotate neighborhoods in a 5, 10, 20 or 50 kb window with profile-profile HHalign or HHsearch.
7. Characterize by structure (local ColabFold, DALI against PDB50 and AFDB50). Extract nucleotide flanks for RNA and repeat work (MEME, GLAM2, minced, Geneious).

**P3. Precomputed system call, then dereplicate and curate.** Nobrega lab 2024–2026, Mutte 2025, Silas AR 2025.
1. Download precomputed calls: PADLOC DB over RefSeq v209 (223,143–223,572 genomes), DefenseFinder RefSeq calls (172,333 genomes), or CDD assignments precomputed for 24,757 genomes. Alternatively run PADLOC v1.1.0 plus DefenseFinder v1.0.8 locally.
2. Filter on "e-value < 1e−10 and coverage of at least 70% of the corresponding PADLOC profile" (50% in the Tmn paper). Drop genomes withdrawn from RefSeq and keep complete systems only (`lit/2026_Wu...Druantia...txt:4408-4433`; `lit/2026_Wu...Tmn...txt:167`).
3. Dereplicate with MMseqs2 `-c 0.9 --min-seq-id 0.98`, or at 90% identity.
4. Build the tree: MUSCLE5, then trimAl gappyout or ClipKIT, then IQ-TREE2 `-B 1000`, then TreeCluster clades.
5. Curate by motif: drop sequences lacking P-loop or Walker A, or a recognisable nuclease domain.
6. Add context from geNomad, TreasureIsland and Platon, in a ±10-gene or 20-kb window.
7. Model with AF2 or AF3. Foldseek against AFDB50 is used once.

**P4. Corpus-scale clustering, then guilt by association.** Millman 2022 and Silas PCD 2025.
1. Cluster every protein: MMseqs2 cascaded over 135 M proteins from 38,167 IMG genomes (`lit/2022_Millman...txt:1911-1917`), or replace each ORF by its cluster ID so a genome becomes "an ordered list of IDs of clustered proteins" (`lit/2025_Silas...PCD...txt:144`).
2. For each cluster, compute the fraction of members within 10 genes of a positive-set gene and test it against a binomial background. Count only members whose contig has 10 genes on each side.
3. Thresholds: ≥4 genera, effective size ≥10, score ≥0.4, q ≤0.05. Refine the positive set once.
4. HHpred/HHblits for domain organization, then experiments.

**P5. Structure first, then sequence census.** TIGR 2025 and the Druantia DruH step.
1. Pick a seed domain structure by the "least common denominator" argument (`lit/2025_Faure...TIGR...txt:41`).
2. Run DALI or Foldseek against AFDB clustered at 50%, MGnify-ESMFold at 50%, and BFVD. Foldseek bitscore > 20 serves as a prefilter, then DALI Z > 6 as the scorer.
3. Mask the DALI alignment to the seed and discard gapped positions.
4. Build an HMM from the structural clade and run hmmsearch over the contig corpus (3,951,425 hits).
5. Cluster in cascades. Fold all 47,428 representatives with local ColabFold. Embed with ESM2-15B, then Leiden clustering, to obtain communities.

**P6. Nucleotide-first discovery.** BhoobalanChitty 2024, Chaban 2024, Urayama 2026, Silas Ronin, Yutin, Streptococci.
- Promoter as seed: take 100 bp upstream of each gene, find a per-genome motif with MEME, rescan with RSAT matrix-scan at p or E < 1e-8. One motif per genome, by design (`lit/2024_BhoobalanChitty...txt:111`).
- Re-call ORFs before searching: NCBI ORFfinder ≥75 bp on 18,382 phage genomes (`lit/2024_Yutin...txt:118`). On circular genomes, six-frame calling with circKit, "stopping after three wrappings" (`lit/2026_Urayama...txt:219`). In eukaryotes, gene re-prediction with AUGUSTUS plus Spaln on ±10 kb (`lit/2023_Jiang...txt:2466`).
- Disagreement between protein and genome search: a pseudogene is called when tBLASTn hits and BLASTp does not (`lit/2022_deCrecyLagard...txt:570`). A gene swap is called when there is protein similarity but no nucleotide similarity (`lit/2024_Yutin...txt:465`).
- CRISPR spacers: run minced on local assemblies, on all RefSeq (May 2023), and on the JGI high-quality spacer DB. Concatenate circular targets twice before the spacer search (`lit/2026_Urayama...txt:265`).

## 6. What runs on web servers, what runs locally

**Consistently on web servers.**
- HHpred (MPI Toolkit) in nearly every paper that uses it. The ESCRT paper says so explicitly: "HHpred search was run on the web against PDB, CDD v3.16, and PFAM_A v36" (`lit/2024_Makarova_Tobiasson...txt:109`).
- DALI server (BhoobalanChitty, Juk, Karamycheva, Urayama; Yutin for structure-guided alignments).
- AlphaFold3 server (TIGR multimer, Urayama RNA-protein, likely YprA/END/Tmn), ColabFold notebook or web (Silas PCD, Karamycheva), COSMIC2 AF2 (Kiwa).
- webFlaGs (END), EFI-EST/GNT (Streptococci SSN), PDBeFold (Millman, StokarAvihail), ARNold (TIGR, Chaban), InterPro, NCBI BLAST/Entrez (GonzalezDelgado, StokarAvihail), IMG neighborhood viewer (DefenseFinder retrons), PADLOC DB download page (Tmn).

**Consistently local.**
- PSI-BLAST, BLASTP/N, tBLASTn, hmmsearch, MMseqs2, CD-HIT, DefenseFinder, PADLOC, CRISPRCasFinder, minced, MEME/RSAT/FIMO, Infernal, trees.
- The heavy structure jobs, where they exist: local ColabFold for 47,428 TIGR representatives and ESM2-15B embeddings (`lit/2025_Faure...TIGR...txt:191-197`); local ColabFold plus DALI v5 against PDB50/AF2DB50 (Tn7); AF2 2.2.0 singularity on NIH BIOWULF plus local pdb70 Dali (`lit/2024_Yutin...txt:228-237`); local AF3 3.0.1 with a custom MSA DB (Urayama).

**The split that recurs.** Per-candidate adjudication runs on web servers: HHpred, DALI, AF3, one protein at a time, on tens to hundreds of proteins. Anything that touches the whole corpus runs locally.

**Compute is almost never reported.** One paper gives numbers: Silas PCD, "74 min" on 9,451 genomes, peak memory 5.682 GB, 2 × 32-core EPYC 9354, fastANI dominant (`lit/2025_Silas...PCD...txt:148`). DefenseFinder gives "less than a day on 5000 genomes on a regular laptop (using 4 CPU)" (`lit/2022_Tesson...txt:2747-2748`). Yutin, Urayama and Silas name clusters (BIOWULF, IU PTI) without wall times. The 47,428-structure TIGR run, the largest job in the slice, has no cost stated.

## 7. Operations a local corpus-wide store must serve fast

These operations recur across papers and are slow or impossible on public services.

1. **Precomputed profile-hit lookup by model ID, with per-hit E-value, bitscore and profile coverage, joined to genome status.** Examples: "sequences matching DUF262/ParB_n profiles (pfam03235 and COG1472) from the conserved domain database (CDD) assignments precalculated for 24,757 completely sequenced genomes" (`lit/2025_Silas...Anti_Restriction...txt:89`), and PADLOC tables filtered at 1e-10/70% with withdrawn genomes dropped. Three Nobrega papers did nothing else to find their system.
2. **A hit-by-all-models score matrix, not only per-model top lists.** Mutte had to score every hit against all 14 helicase HMMs and assign by fold difference, because TIGRFAM and custom HMMs over-call: 27,846 RecB hits (`lit/2025_Mutte...txt:1493-1500`).
3. **Anchor to neighborhood fetch with predicates.** Window in genes (±5/±10/±50) or bp (5/10/20/50 kb), strand, intergenic distance < 50 bp, distance to contig end, and circular topology. Every pattern above uses this, often at each clustering level (YprA).
4. **Neighbor annotation in bulk.** PSI-BLAST against CDD PSSMs, Pfam/COG/arCOG hits and MMseqs2 cluster IDs for every gene in every window. Precomputed annotation plus cluster IDs answers this directly.
5. **Iterative PSI-BLAST or jackhmmer from a PSSM or MSA against 25 k–50 k complete genomes, and clustered NR, with a fixed `-Z`.** Hits must come back with locus coordinates so context curation can run between rounds.
6. **Cascaded MMseqs2 clustering of hit sets** (0.5 → 0.75 → 0.9; 100/30 → 70/70 → 50/70) and dereplication at 0.98/0.9.
7. **Domain-envelope extraction and re-search.** Pull the RuvC or CTD envelope from every hit and search with it alone (Fanzor, END "The iENDOIII CTD domain was further ran against the clustered NR", `lit/2026_Yee...txt:165`).
8. **Six-frame or ORF-level search of contigs with protein/genome disagreement.** tBLASTn hits without BLASTp hits flag pseudogenes or missed ORFs. Unannotated stop-to-stop ORFs > 100 aa are searched. Gene starts are re-called (the "Start_position(amended)" column in BhoobalanChitty MOESM4).
9. **Flank and IGR extraction at scale.** 100 bp upstream (promoters); −30..+1 around TSSs; first exon −1.5 kb and last exon +1.5 kb (Fanzor RNA); 5 kb flanks for repeat and RNA motif discovery (TIGR); CRISPR arrays and spacers corpus-wide.
10. **Genome-collection statistics.** ORF-pair co-occurrence matrices with ±10-gene windows (Silas PCD); defense-score enrichment over all clusters (Millman); presence/absence profiles with tree-based genome weights (CRISPR-Cas, Karamycheva); all-pairs ANI.
11. **Structure-scale screening.** Foldseek prefilter then DALI against AFDB50/MGnify-ESM/BFVD, and bulk folding of tens of thousands of representatives. This is the one corpus-wide structure workload in the slice (TIGR).

## 8. Failure modes authors explicitly guarded against

- **Profile drift and the "banana conundrum".** "iterative searches of genomic neighborhoods rarely converge ... hundreds or thousands of neighbors after several iterations" and "using acr gene neighbors as seeds can result in fast departure from the acr context" (`lit/2023_Makarova_Wolf_Koonin_In_Silico_Prediction_Anti_CRISPR.txt:37`). Countermeasures seen:
  - fixed round counts;
  - realigning with the original seeds each round (Fanzor);
  - the >98%-retained / ≤10-new-false-positives acceptance test, with false positives defined as hits outside CRISPR-cas islands (`lit/2025_Makarova...txt:141-147`);
  - per-genome motifs instead of pooled ones (BhoobalanChitty);
  - reciprocal PSI-BLAST from the borderline region (Iyer `:62`).
- **Promiscuous domains and paralogs.**
  - Cas3/CARF are exempted from the false-positive count "given the abundance of homologues outside CRISPR-Cas systems".
  - AAA-ATPase cargo is mistaken for TnsC (Tn7 `:2073`).
  - In antiSMASH, isocitrate-lyase paralogs are suppressed by negative HMMs with higher bitscore cutoffs.
  - Vps4 is delimited from other AAA+ ATPases by MIT domain or ESCRT neighbors (ESCRT `:29`).
  - SEFIR is distinguished from TIR by comparing HHsearch strength and position (Millman).
  - Helicase over-calling is handled by all-vs-all model scoring (Mutte).
- **Multidomain proteins and fusions.**
  - Envelopes are trimmed using AF2 cores ("restricted to the core region of TniQ ... roughly corresponding to the first 300aa", Tn7 `:2114`).
  - Fused Cas3/Cas5/Cas11 domains are located with Foldseek and deleted before comparing Cas8/Cas10 (CRISPR-Cas `:175`).
  - Fused N- and C-terminal domains are removed from JukA before the tree (Juk `:161`).
  - Domains are searched one by one (END; TnsF DALI per domain).
  - Two profile sets are kept for effectors, full-length and domain-only (Cas12/Cas13).
- **Gene-call errors: false fusions and splits, missed ORFs, wrong starts.**
  - Natural split KwaB is concatenated, not discarded (Kiwa).
  - KwaA proteins with an unexpected TM count are checked manually for start-codon misannotation (Kiwa `:2490-2491`).
  - Partial genes at contig ends are dropped; annotations are refreshed against RefSeq v227.
  - "Of 100 complete genomes retrieved, ronA homologs had been annotated in 84" (`lit/2025_Silas...PCD...txt:158`).
  - Riboswitch tandem calls exclude gaps > 300 nt as likely unannotated ORFs (`lit/2022_Sherlock...txt:26`).
  - QueF is reclassified as an inactive QueC-QueF fusion, and the authors warn that "pseudogenes can create false positive hits that can skew the pathway predictions" (`lit/2022_deCrecyLagard...txt:704`).
- **Clonal and sampling inflation.** Tree-based genome weights (CRISPR-Cas `:169`, Karamycheva `:146`); MMseqs2 or CD-HIT dereplication before counting (nearly every paper); the Pagel test on a Mash tree instead of raw counts (Wu 2024 `:2576-2581`); fastANI ≥85% filters and 90%-gene-overlap pruning of redundant regions (Silas PCD `:144-146`); exclusion of clades with mean pairwise distance < 0.1 (Urayama `:227`); BLASTCLUST "L = 0.9 and S = 1.89" (Iyer `:148`).
- **E-values not comparable across databases or families.** Fanzor fixes `-Z`. The anti-CRISPR review states the general case: "using the same search parameters for different protein families leads to considerable over- or under-detection of homologs for many of those families" (`lit/2023_Makarova_Wolf...txt:83`). Karamycheva tunes the cutoff per seed (1e-5, 1e-67, 1e-22, 1e-61) and stops when the last hit loses its locus context. Yutin avoids E-values entirely for genome distance, using a coverage-based formula.
- **Contaminated or truncated assemblies.** Fanzor discards proteins from contig-level assemblies or contigs < 50 kb (`:2481-2484`); Wu 2024 removes long-branch genomes (TreeShrink), CheckM contamination > 5 and metadata-less genomes; Millman and Vassallo exclude short contigs from neighborhood statistics; Urayama excludes cccRNA candidates by nt/nr BLAST and coverage filters.
- **Low-complexity and single-feature structure similarity.** Urayama dismissed 4 of 18,609 HHblits hits and abandoned Foldseek clustering because it clustered on single helices (`:225`). TIGR discarded a whole Leiden community as "false-positive selection during clustering" (`:203`). AF2 regions below pLDDT 50 are ignored; DALI hits are verified by hand in PyMOL (Tn7 `:2105-2109`).
- **Rule-based callers miss things.** "Neither DefenseFinder nor PADLOC identified this gene as a defense system" (`lit/2026_Yee...txt:23`). DefenseFinder deliberately leaves 15% of REBASE type IV undetected to protect specificity (`lit/2022_Tesson...txt:2915-2919`). Sensitive HMM scans missed the most divergent potyvirid modules, which were then found by eye (`lit/2026_Lee...txt:97`).
- **Negative and ambiguous results reported rather than hidden.** No methyltransferase next to END, so it is not a canonical R-M system; no conserved Tmn operon; Pin integration sites and IntN1 repeats not found in the new Bacteroidota loci (`lit/2026_Karamycheva...txt:108`); spacer targets unidentifiable because TIGR systems are mostly metagenomic (`lit/2025_Faure...TIGR...txt:101`).

## 10. Consequences for the plan

- The store must expose precomputed profile hits (Pfam/NCBIfam/CDD/COG/arCOG/PADLOC/DefenseFinder) as a queryable table with per-hit E-value, bitscore, profile coverage and locus coordinates. Three papers in this slice did their entire census that way, and a fourth needed an all-models score matrix per hit.
- Neighborhood fetch must take a window in genes or bp, a strand or operon predicate and a contig-edge policy, because papers differ on every one of these and state them as methods. The default policies worth implementing are ±10 genes, ±5 kb / 20 kb, co-directional with < 50 bp spacing, and the three edge behaviors seen (exclude within N kb, truncate, require N genes each side), plus circular topology for phage and viroid-like genomes.
- Iterative search must keep locus context attached to hits between rounds, because curation between rounds is by tree plus neighborhood in every careful paper, not by E-value.
- Fixed `-Z` (or equivalent) should be the default so E-values stay comparable as the corpus grows; per-family threshold tuning should be recorded with the query, since the literature does this by hand and says so.
- Domain-envelope extraction and re-search is a first-class operation, not a postprocessing step.
- Keep HHpred, DALI, AF3 and webFlaGs as external per-candidate services. Keep Foldseek-prefilter-then-DALI and bulk folding local, since that is the only corpus-wide structure workload anyone ran.
- Acceptance tests should use candidates 1–9 above: recall and precision against the published hit list, at the stage where the target taxa first appear.
