# The goal, the order, and the register of what is deferred

Started 2026-11-20 at the PI's word (DEC-59). This file is the one place that says what the corpus is meant to
hold in the end, why sources are taken in the order they are, and, for every selection made on the way, what was
left out and how to go back for it. **Nothing in this file is a decision to leave a source out for good.** Keep it
current: a card that selects, filters or dereplicates adds its row here in the same commit as its note.

Numbers carry the labels of AGENTS §6. Those marked *(card)* are copied from the card or note named beside them and
were not measured again for this file.

## 1. The goal

In the PI's words (2026-11-20): "the ultimate goal is a COMPLETE catalog across all useful seuqeced data", and "i do
want to go and pick up less diverse sources in full later".

So:

- **The end state is completeness**: every useful sequenced genome, MAG, plasmid, virus and assembled metagenome
  contig, with its neighbourhood, not a diverse sample of them.
- **Diversity first is an order of work, not a scope.** Sources are ranked by new protein families (NR50 clusters)
  per Gbp because storage and CPU are short now (STATUS open item 19). A source ranked low is taken later, in full.
- **Every selection is reversible on paper.** A step that takes representatives, dereplicates, filters by length or
  stops a habitat early records what it did not take, with identifiers sufficient to fetch and ingest the remainder
  later (accessions, sample ids, the archive member, the filter's parameters), in its measurement note and in §4
  below.
- **What "complete" covers (DEC-60, 2026-11-20; expanded by DEC-69, 2026-11-23).** For unbinned metagenome
  assemblies: every contig of 5,000 bp or more; unbinned metagenome contigs under 5 kb are outside the corpus and are
  not deferred data. Dedicated and niche collections — including `< 5 kb` circular DNA viruses (*Cressdnaviricota*,
  *Microviridae*), archaeal extremophile viruses, phage satellites (`PICIs`, `cf-PICIs`, `PLEs`, `P4`-like), curated
  integrative MGEs/transposons/IS elements/integrons, single-cell SAGs, genome-reduced endosymbionts, post-`R226`
  prokaryotes, niche-habitat/holobiont MAG compendia, organellar genomes (plastids, mitochondria, nucleomorphs),
  microbial eukaryotes (by design card C-60), and RNA viruses plus Obelisks and viroid-like circular RNAs (by design
  card C-50) — are in scope without the unbinned 5 kb floor. Protein-only catalogues are deferred ("can wait"). Raw
  reads are not taken (Logan's assemblies stand in for them, §4.3).
- **Where it lives** is the main constraint (STATUS open item 19). The pod's local disk holds the working root; the
  PI's NAS `tower` is offered as a place for tranches as the corpus grows (DEC-60 (3), card C-49).

## 2. The order of work, and why

Measured yield, new NR50 clusters per Gbp (*derived* from the totals in each note; the SPIRE figures come from
`linclust` on the pilot and the others from the release clustering, so compare them loosely):

|---|---|---|

| SPIRE unbinned contigs, aquatic | 465,323 | card C-42, `novelty/novelty_by_habitat1.tsv` (*measured*, contigs of 1.5 kb and more) |
| SPIRE unbinned, anthropogenic | 425,040 | the same |

| Plasmids from isolates (`PL1`) | about 34,000 | 200,698 new clusters over 5,924,552,875 bp (card C-35) |

Of the pilot's 41,282,757 new clusters, 9,466,693 (23%) have more than one member (*measured*, card C-42): part of
the unbinned novelty is fragments. Yield fell between the first and second half of the pilot's samples in every
large habitat (for example terrestrial 465,288 to 349,345 per Gbp), so each habitat has a point where the next
tranche stops paying; at that point the habitat is **deferred, not finished** (§4).

The order that follows (the orchestrator's, 2026-11-20, for the PI to change):

1. High-diversity & ART-RT-enriched viral/plasmid stages on disk (§3; `DEC-67`, `DEC-70`, `DEC-71`):
   - `G1 + SPX1 -> r26.11.3` (building now);
   - **`V2 + PL2 + PL3 + CG1` (plus `MV1` if ready in time, else leading `r26.11.5`) -> `r26.11.4`** (`DEC-71`: pulls in already-on-pod `CGVR` `120,568` gut viral bins `CG1` and `MetaVR` `24.4M` UViGs `MV1` alongside `IMG/VR` `V2` and conjugative plasmids `PL2`+`PL3`, while deferring eukaryotic giant viruses `GV1` behind the ART-RT/long-read wave and keeping strain-rich `C1`..`C4` and `C6` in item 6).
2. **ART-RT & intact-neighborhood priority wave (`r26.11.5+`, `DEC-70`, `DEC-71`)**:
   - **`MV1` (`MetaVR` non-redundant Complete/HQ/MQ vOTU reps + all jumbo/large UViGs `>= 30 kb`, `pyrodigal-gv`, card C-62)** if not already cut in `r26.11.4`;
   - **Public long-read (`PACBIO_SMRT` and `OXFORD_NANOPORE`) metagenome assemblies (`LR1`.., card C-56)** called with **`pyrodigal-gv` (`--genecall-mode gv-meta`, card B-67)** and enriched first for soil/*Myxococcota*, cyanobacterial/freshwater, and activated-sludge/wastewater HiFi assemblies (eliminating the `38.2%` Illumina tandem-array collapse rate and preserving TAG/TGA-recoded jumbophage ORFs);
   - **Curated integrative MGEs/ICEs/prophages (`MGE1`, card C-57)**, **phage satellites + post-`14Apr2025` `INPHARED` jumbo-phage delta + `VIRE` (`SAT1`/`V3`, card C-55)**, and **basal proto-ART bacterial MAG phyla (`G_ART1`: *Myxococcota*, *Cyanobacteriota*, *Omnitrophota*, *Planctomycetota*, *Patescibacteriota*/CPR from on-pod `GlobDB r226`, card C-63)**.
3. Remaining viral, giant-virus, organellar, and unbinned short-read metagenome waves (`GV1` GVMAGs V2 already staged; `< 5 kb` CRESS/*Microviridae* and archaeal viruses in `C-55`; RefSeq organelles `O1` in `C-59`; full IMG/VR v4.0 viral vOTU representatives; and `SPX2`.. short-read metagenomes `>= 5 kb` called with `gv-meta` under `B-67`, followed by IMG/M, MGnify, and INSDC).
4. Remaining species representatives and niche cellular clades: GTDB R226 (`S3`, `S5`), remaining GlobDB tranches (`G2`..`G41` less `G_ART1`), uncultivated Single-Amplified Genomes (`GORG-Tropics`, `GORG-Dark`), CheckM-excluded endosymbionts and post-`R226` RefSeq/GenBank prokaryotes, and niche-habitat/holobiont MAG compendia (`DEC-69`, card C-58).
5. RNA viruses, Obelisks, and viroid-like circular RNAs once card C-50 settles RNA genome calling/tokens, and microbial
   eukaryotes (`IMG-UMAG` `E1`/`E2`, `Tara` `SMAGs`, `EukProt`, `PhycoCosm`, `MycoCosm`) once card C-60 settles
   intron-aware eukaryotic calling.
6. Then the strain-rich and less diverse remainder of every source, in full (`C1`..`C4` and `C6`, plus §4).
