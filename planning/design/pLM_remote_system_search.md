# Protein & Genomic Language Models for Remote Homolog and Conserved System Search in `bio-box`

**Status:** Primary-source research & architecture planning note (zero code implementation, zero pod compute, zero sequence downloads).
**Scope:** Evaluates protein language models (pLMs), genomic/operon language models (gLMs), and 1D structural-alphabet translators (`3Di`) against `bio-box`'s existing sequence/profile/neighborhood architecture (`SEARCH_PLAN.md`, `C-38_import_depths.md`, `src/biobox/registry/schemas.py`, `AGENTS.md` §3–§7), anchored to the live `r26.11.1` release (`59.88 Gbp`), the on-disk `~0.92 Tbp` harvest (`C-48a`), and the `~14.6 Tbp` global metagenome horizon.

**Number Labels (`AGENTS.md` §6):** Every quantitative claim below carries one of four explicit labels:

## Executive Summary

1. **What Laub's `DefensePredictor` actually is (and is not):** Contrary to single-protein pLM summaries, DeWeirdt, Mahoney & Laub (2026, *Science* 392:eadv7924, `PMC13092281`) is a **5-gene local-context tabular classifier**: it runs frozen `esm2_t30_150M_UR50D` (layer 30, 640-dim mean-pooled, truncated at 1,022 aa), concatenates the center gene with its `±2` same-contig neighbors (`3,200` dims), appends `119` nucleotide/strand/distance features (including genome-wide z-scored GC and dinucleotides), and averages log-odds across a 5-fold LightGBM ensemble (`PeterDeWeirdt/defense_predictor`, `core.py:62-168, 436-495`). It validated **42 / 94 (45%)** predicted *E. coli* transcriptional units (TUs) vs **1 / 24 (4.2%)** HHblits-matched low-score controls, proving pLMs can discriminate defensive from non-defensive members within shared superfamilies (e.g., PD-(D/E)XK nucleases, P-loop NTPases). However, its CV split held out **`>30%` AA identity (`qcov > 0.8`)** via MMseqs2 profile search (`05_04_02_split_data.ipynb`), **not `<20%` ID / `hmmsearch E > 10` / structural homology**; and its contig-edge imputation sentinels (`0`, `1.1`, `2`, `-200`) plus per-genome GC z-scoring (`core.py:158-166, 440-473`) degrade on fragmented `1.5–5 kb` metagenomic contigs.
2. **The back-to-back *Science* 2026 contrast (`ESM_DF` + `GeneCLR_DF`):** Mordret, Bernheim et al. (2026, *Science* `10.1126/science.adv8275`; `mdmparis/defense-finder`) pair a LoRA-finetuned ESM2 (`r=16, alpha=32`, SwiGLU mean-pool head) with `GeneCLR_DF`, a **12-layer, 8-head, 480-dim Double-Stranded Attention Transformer** over ESM2-35M embeddings with `asinh(0.03 * d)` inter-gene distance biases (`finetuning_config_minimal.yaml:6-27`). This validates tape-level contextual transformers as a viable pattern, while also confirming that raw `ESM2-35M` (`480` dim) suffices as a context-track input.
3. **Why corpus-wide dense embeddings are the wrong default for `bio-box`:** At `r26.11.1` (`9.29M` `NR50` reps), dense `fp16` vectors (`1,024` dim) cost `19.0 GB` and `~15–40 GPU-h`, which is small. At `~14.6 Tbp` (`1.0–1.5B` `NR50` reps), dense `fp16` vectors balloon to **`2.0–3.1 TB`**, break `wilkcomp02`'s `200 GB` RAM cap unless heavily quantized (`FAISS IVF-PQ` / `RaBitQ` binary/4-bit), and still suffer **multi-domain dilution** on fused defense proteins (e.g., `NACHT+effector`, `TniQ+nuclease`). Meanwhile, **1D `3Di` strings** (`ProstT5` / `ESM-2 3B 3Di`, `SD-21`) require only `~120 B/ORF` zstd (`1.1 GB` at `r26.11.1`; `120–180 GB` at `1.0–1.5B` `NR50`), support local Smith–Waterman alignment on multi-domain fusions natively via Foldseek, and run inside `wilkcomp02`'s CPU/RAM envelope (`--sort-by-structure-bits 0`: `~0.65 kB/entry`).
4. **Recommended Hybrid Architecture (Options A + D, with gated Option C):**
   - **Corpus-wide (`NR50` pack):** Commit to **Option A (`SD-21` `3Di` strings on `NR50` reps)** once the `SD-21` bake-off picks between `ProstT5` (built into Foldseek v10, no PyTorch runtime needed) and `ESM-2 3B 3Di`.
   - **On-demand rounds (`round=<id>`, `C-38e/f/g` pattern):** Adopt **Option D** for high-cost per-residue/pLM inference (`pLM-BLAST` / `EBA`, `DefensePredictor`/`GeneCLR_DF`, `ESM-C 300M/600M` embeddings) strictly on content-addressed candidate subsets: `±10`-gene hit neighborhoods, recurrent unannotated `NR50` cluster reps (`size >= K`, `DEC-54 Q14`), and MGE-enriched / CRISPR-proximal `dark_orf`s — **never** bulk-embedding raw `dark_orf`s (`123.44M` in `r26.11.1`).
   - **Gated tape-context classifier (Option C):** Only build a `contig_tapes`-native operon/defense-island scorer (consuming `NR90` rep vectors + `dist_to_contig_end_bp` edge-censoring + Pfam/CRISPR/MGE badges) **if** it clears the falsifiable 1-GPU bake-off gate on `r26.11.1` (§4) with strictly `<20%` ID and `hmmsearch E > 10` holdouts.

### 1.2 Architecture, Embeddings, Pooling & Genomic Context Representation

Inspected directly from `PeterDeWeirdt/defense_predictor` (`defense_predictor/core.py`) and `PeterDeWeirdt/defense_predictor_manuscript` (`code/11_01_get_protein_embeddings.py`, `code/11_05_00_cross_validate_def_pred.py`) plus `PMC13092281` Results:

- **pLM Backbone & Layer:** Frozen **`esm2_t30_150M_UR50D`** (`150M` parameters, `30` transformer layers, `640`-dim hidden size; *published*, `core.py:98-102`, `11_01_get_protein_embeddings.py:11-14`). No fine-tuning of ESM2 weights.
- **Extraction & Pooling:** Final layer (`repr_layer = 30`) token representations are **mean-pooled** across sequence positions `1 : min(1022, len(seq)) + 1` (excluding `<cls>` and `<eos>` tokens; sequences `>1,022` aa are **hard-truncated** at 1,022 aa; *published*, `core.py:101, 125-128`, `11_01_get_protein_embeddings.py:13, 35-37`). Batching uses `FastaBatchedDataset` with `toks_per_batch = 4096` (`core.py:100, 110-114`). No per-residue alignment or domain-window pooling is performed.
- **Multi-Gene Genomic Context (`±2` CDS Window):** `DefensePredictor` is **NOT** a single-protein classifier. For every center CDS (filtering out non-CDS and `pseudo` rows, `core.py:36-38`), `get_neighbor_df` extracts up to **`n_neighbors = 2`** upstream and downstream CDSs on the **same `genomic_accession`** (`core.py:62-72`). When `center_strand == '-'`, relative positions are sign-flipped (`-2, -1, 0, +1, +2` so `-1` is always 5' upstream; `core.py:76-77`).
- **3,319-Dimensional Feature Matrix (`PMC13092281` Results; `core.py:436-476`):**
  1. **Protein pLM embeddings (`3,200` features):** Mean-pooled `640`-dim ESM2 vectors for positions `-2, -1, 0, +1, +2` concatenated (`5 × 640 = 3,200`).
  2. **Nucleotide composition (`105` features = `5 × 21`):** For each of the 5 genes, GC fraction (`gc_frac`), 4 mononucleotide fractions (`A, C, T, G`), and 16 dinucleotide fractions (`AA..GG`), **each z-scored across all CDSs in the input genome** (`_compute_motif_features`, `core.py:136-168`).
  3. **Protein lengths (`5` features):** Amino-acid length of each gene in the `±2` window (`core.py:260-274, 454-461`).
  4. **Strand co-directionality (`5` features):** Binary indicator `strand == center_strand` for each position `-2..+2` (`core.py:277-290, 463-469`; note position `0` is identically `1`).
  5. **Inter-gene distances (`4` features):** `next.start - curr.end` for adjacent pairs `dist_-2:-1`, `dist_-1:0`, `dist_0:1`, `dist_1:2`, with basic wraparound handling when `end < start` (`core.py:293-331, 471-473`).
  *(Arithmetic check: `3,200 + 105 + 5 + 5 + 4 = 3,319`, matching `PMC13092281` Results paragraph 25 exactly, `*derived*`.)*
- **Contig-Edge Missing-Value Imputation (`core.py:440-473`):** Critical for `bio-box` metagenomics — when a neighbor does not exist on the same contig, `DefensePredictor` fills with hard-coded sentinels: **`0`** for ESM2 embeddings (`core.py:440`) and protein lengths (`core.py:460`), **`1.1`** for z-scored nucleotide motifs (`core.py:451`), **`2`** for co-directionality (`core.py:468`), and **`-200`** for inter-gene distances (`core.py:473`).
- **What Drives Predictions (SHAP Analysis, `PMC13092281` Fig. 1C–D, Table S3):** Center-gene ESM2 features dominate total positive SHAP attribution across `10,307` true positives; **center-gene z-scored GC content** is the #1 non-ESM2 feature (#4 overall), exploiting the lower-GC horizontal-transfer signature of defense genes (`PMC13092281` Fig. 1D, fig. S1A). Neighbor-gene SHAP attributions are enriched for known defense proteins, transposases, and integrases (`fig. S1B`), confirming the LightGBM trees implicitly learn both defense-island and MGE-neighborhood signals. Ablation: nearest-neighbor cosine similarity on center-gene ESM2 embeddings alone yields **AP = 0.51** vs **0.86** for full `DefensePredictor` (`PMC13092281` Results), proving that `±2` neighbor context + GC/operon features + supervised tree interaction add **`+0.35` AP**.

### 1.3 Training Sets, Homology Holdout Stringency & Score Calibration

- **Positive Set (`PMC13092281` Results; `code/03_00_defensefinder_search.py`):** DefenseFinder run across `~17,000` taxonomically representative RefSeq prokaryotic genomes → `~244,000` homologs belonging to **complete** defense systems → clustered with MMseqs2 at **`30%` sequence identity and `80%` reciprocal coverage** (`1` random protein per cluster) → **`~15,000` defense proteins** (`*published*`).
- **Negative Set (`PMC13092281` Results; `code/04_03_get_background_seqs.ipynb`):** `~14,000,000` RefSeq genes carrying non-defensive GO or COG annotations (translation, membrane transport, etc.) plus secreted protein homologs (`secreted_homologs.csv`), excluding any DefenseFinder profile whose `defense_percent > 0.4` (`04_03_get_background_seqs.ipynb`), then clustered at `30%` ID / `80%` coverage → **`~186,000` control proteins** (`*published*`).
- **Homology Holdout Split — CRITICAL AUDIT FINDING (`code/05_03_01_run_mmseqs_profile.py`, `code/05_04_02_split_data.ipynb`):**
  - Authors built 3-iteration MMseqs2 profiles (`-s 6 --num-iterations 3`) against `minimal_seqs`, then ran all-vs-all profile-sequence search (`-s 7.5`) across the `~200,000` candidate sequences (`05_03_01_run_mmseqs_profile.py:14-36`).
  - **Crucially**, `05_04_02_split_data.ipynb` filters edges at **`qcov > 0.8` AND `fident > 0.3`** (`cov_cutoff = 0.8; ident_cutoff = 0.3`), runs Louvain community detection on the filtered graph, merges DefenseFinder exchangeable-gene edges (`exchange_edge_df`) via connected components, and splits into 5 CV folds (`*published*`).
  - **Assessment against `bio-box`'s `<20%` ID & `hmmsearch E > 10` bar:** `DefensePredictor` did **NOT** enforce a `<20%` AA identity, `hmmsearch E > 10`, or Foldseek structural-superfamily holdout. Because edges below `30%` identity or `<80%` query coverage (including partial multi-domain matches!) were dropped before fold assignment, remote sequence and structural homologs spanned folds — which is why on their own held-out folds (`PMC13092281` Fig. 1B), **HMMs scored AP = 0.25**, **BLAST scored AP = 0.29**, and **ProstT5+Foldseek scored AP = 0.38**, against **guilt-by-association AP = 0.63** (AUROC `0.91`), **ESM2 cosine NN AP = 0.51**, and **DefensePredictor AP = 0.86** (AUROC `0.97`).
- **Training & Calibration (`code/11_05_00_cross_validate_def_pred.py:57-164`; `core.py:480-495`):**
  - 5-fold CV LightGBM (`LGBMClassifier`); inside each training fold, a 10% cluster-grouped validation split (`GroupKFold(n_splits=10)`) tunes `num_leaves ∈ [4, 256]` and `min_child_samples ∈ [32, 1024]` over 15 Optuna TPE trials maximizing **Average Precision** (`lr=0.1`, `n_estimators=5,000`, early stopping `50`). Final fold models train with `lr=0.01`, `n_estimators=30,000`, early stopping `200` on AP.
  - Inference averages per-fold **log-odds** `log(p / (1 - p))` across all 5 pickled models (`beaker_fold_0..4.pkl`, `core.py:485-491`), reporting `mean_log_odds`, `sd_log_odds`, `min_log_odds`, `max_log_odds`. No Platt scaling or isotonic post-calibration is applied; instead, `mean_log_odds >= 4` (`p >= 0.982`) is recommended as the stringent discovery threshold (`README.md:62`; `PMC13092281` Results).

### 1.5 Wet-Lab Validation, False-Positive Classes & Blind Spots

- **Experimental Validation (`PMC13092281` Results, Fig. 3–5, Table S6–S8):**
  - **94 predicted TUs** (not called by DefenseFinder; `38` random + `56` curated for novelty/score; `13` remote homologs, `8` structural homologs, `73` uncharacterized) cloned with native promoters on a low-copy plasmid in *E. coli* MG1655 and challenged with **24 diverse *E. coli* phages**.
  - **Criteria:** smaller plaque size or `>= 10-fold` EOP reduction vs empty vector.
  - **Yield:** **`42 / 94` active (`45%`)** — `32 / 73` uncharacterized (`43.8%`, *derived*), `3 / 8` structural homologs (`37.5%`), `7 / 13` remote homologs (`53.8%`). Validation rate tracked `mean_log_odds` tightly (`Pearson r = 0.95`, Fig. 3B).
  - **Negative Control Arm:** **`24` TUs** with 3-iter HHblits homology to known defense proteins (including `6` PD-(D/E)XK nucleases) but `mean_log_odds < 0` → only **`1 / 24` active (`4.2%`, *derived*)**. Across all `118` tested TUs at `log-odds = 0`, DefensePredictor achieved **45% precision / 98% recall (AP = 0.62)** vs HHblits **40% precision / 74% recall (AP = 0.42)** (`p < 0.01` McNemar for recall).
  - **Novel Biochemical Mechanisms Validated by Active-Site Mutagenesis (Fig. 5):** `5` DUFs (`DUF3037`, `DUF1829`, `DUF5830`, `DUF6988`, `DUF6650`) and `10` defined domains newly validated in defense, including **`DS-8`** (metallophosphatase homologous to human cGAMP-degrading SMPDL3A + NACHT NTPase), **`DS-9`** (metallophosphatase + HAD phosphatase), **`DS-11`** (adenine-binding CBS domain + HEPN RNase, Y157A ablates defense), **`DS-6`** (HipA-like kinase + DUF3037/DUF1829 antitoxin), **`DS-2`** (CBASS phospholipase+cGAS + essential di-adenylate cyclase), **`DS-3`** (split-PIN RNase), and **`DS-5`** (PIN RNase + AcrIF4-like/DUF5830 non-TA partner).
- **Characterized False-Positive Classes (`52 / 94 = 55%` unvalidated in the *E. coli* screen) & Failure Modes:**
  1. **Promiscuous catalytic superfamilies:** Among the 168 uncharacterized ECOR clusters, the top domains are **PD-(D/E)XK nucleases (9%)**, **P-loop NTPases (5%)**, and **RHH DNA-binding domains (4%)** (Fig. 2D); while DefensePredictor rejects many non-defensive PD-(D/E)XK homologs (`1/24` control arm), housekeeping helicases/NTPases, Rossmann methyltransferases, and nucleotide-modification enzymes remain high-risk FP attractors when they sit in low-GC MGEs.
  2. **MGE / defense-island guilt-by-proximity leakage:** Because neighbor SHAP weights reward adjacent transposases, integrases, and known defense genes (`fig. S1B`), neutral passenger genes inside prophages/plasmids/defense islands receive inflated scores (`42%` of multi-gene TUs also had mixed positive/negative genes across the operon).
  3. **Expression/host/phage-panel misses (experimental FNs masquerading as FPs):** Authors note some of the 52 unvalidated TUs may require native hosts, broader phage panels, or wider operon boundaries (`PMC13092281` Discussion).
- **False-Negative Blind Spots (`PMC13092281` Fig. 1E & Discussion):**
  1. **Prospective FNR on 100 newly deposited DefenseFinder systems (1,000 RefSeq genomes):** `82/100` detected at `log-odds > 0` (**18% FNR**) and `68/100` at `log-odds > 4` (**32% FNR**).
  2. **Complete blindness to non-coding RNAs:** Cannot detect CRISPR arrays, retron ncRNAs, or defensive sRNAs/riboswitches because inputs are CDS translations only (`PMC13092281` Discussion).
  3. **Taxonomic training bias:** Training positives are heavily skewed toward *E. coli* / Gammaproteobacteria (`DefenseFinder`); performance in archaea, CPR/DPANN, and divergent environmental phyla is untested.
  4. **Length & fragmentation failure modes:** Hard `1,022`-aa truncation cuts C-terminal effector domains in giant multi-domain defense proteins (e.g., Type I/III RM, Druantia, Shedu/Zorya, giant phage proteins); genome-wide GC/dinucleotide z-scoring (`core.py:158-166`) and sentinel filling (`0 / 1.1 / 2 / -200`) break on `1.5–5 kb` unbinned metagenomic contigs.

## Section 2. Comparative Full-Text Literature & Database Survey via OpenAlex

Using the OpenAlex API (`https://api.openalex.org/works`, citation graph around `W7148346046` + paradigm searches, zero PII/email) and Europe PMC OA full-text XMLs (`PMC10991518`, `PMC11616678`, `PMC11754643`, `PMC11180608`, `PMC10981738`, `PMC10576641`, `PMC10792726`, `PMC11701548`) plus public GitHub repositories (`mdmparis/defense-finder`, `nebiolabs/domainator`), we dissected all four competing paradigms.

### 2.1 Paradigm (a): Genomic & Operon Context Language Models

- **`gLM` (Hwang et al. 2024, *Nat Commun* 15:2880, `PMC10991518`):** 19-layer, 190-head Transformer (`1,280`-dim hidden state) taking precomputed frozen **ESM2-650M (`1,280`-dim)** protein embeddings + binary strand orientation over **`15–30`-gene** metagenomic contig windows (`7M` MGnify contigs; trained `560` epochs on `4× A100` GPUs). Learns contextual "polysemy" (e.g., distinguishing methanogenic vs methanotrophic MCR complexes) and co-regulation/operon boundaries, but requires contigs with `>= 15` genes — failing on fragmented `1.5–5 kb` contigs (CAMI median `1.33` genes/contig, `C-38` N9).
- **`gLM2` (Cornman et al. 2024/2025, bioRxiv; HuggingFace `tattabio/gLM2_150M` & `gLM2_650M`):** Mixed-modality masked language model (`30%` masking) trained on **`OMG`** (`3.1 Tbp`, `3.3B` CDS from JGI IMG + EMBL MGnify) using **per-AA tokens for CDSs + per-nt tokens for intergenic sequences (IGS)** with `<+>`/`<->` strand tokens inside a **`4,096`-token** context window (`~8–12` short genes or `~3–4` average genes + IGRs, `*derived*`). Uniquely captures non-coding regulatory/RNA elements (promoters, riboswitches, CRISPR repeats) alongside proteins, but `4,096` tokens is far narrower than a `±10`-gene (`~20 kb`) defense island (`~6,800` AA + IGR nt).
- **`GeneCLR_DF` + `ESM_DF` (Mordret, Bernheim et al. 2026, *Science* `10.1126/science.adv8275`; `mdmparis/defense-finder`):** Back-to-back *Science* companion to Laub's `DefensePredictor`. Inspected directly from `defense_finder/ESM_DF/model.py` and `defense_finder/GeneCLR_DF/finetuning_config_minimal.yaml`:
  - **`ESM_DF`** (`model.py:33-141`): Fine-tunes ESM2 (`35M`/`650M`) with **rsLoRA (`r=16, alpha=32`)** on Q/K/V/O projections + a **SwiGLU residual mean-pooling classification head**.
  - **`GeneCLR_DF`** (`finetuning_config_minimal.yaml:5-28`): **12-layer, 8-head Transformer (`hidden_dim = 480`, matching `ESM2-35M`)** with **Double-Stranded Attention (`attention_type: "ds"`)** injecting 6 inter-gene distance/strand bias features transformed via `asinh(0.03 * d)`. Experimentally validated novel antiphage systems in both *Escherichia* and *Streptomyces* (showing cross-phylum generalization into Actinomycetota). Directly validates that **compact `480`-dim `ESM2-35M` embeddings + coordinate/strand attention over a gene tape** are sufficient for operon-level defense discovery.

### 2.2 Paradigm (b): 1D Structural-Alphabet pLMs (`3Di`) + Local Structural Alignment

- **`ProstT5` (Heinzinger et al. 2024, *NAR GAB* `lqae150`, `PMC11616678`):** Bilingual encoder-decoder (`ProtT5` 1.2B encoder fine-tuned on AFDB `3Di` + CNN head for fast `AA -> 3Di`). Achieves SCOPe40 superfamily ROC-AUC **`0.47`** (encoder+CNN `3Di`) vs **`0.49`** (experimental-structure `3Di`) vs **`0.06`** (`MMseqs2`), at **`41` proteins/s** (`1,787` *M. jannaschii* proteins in `43.5 s`) on 1× RTX A5000 GPU (`0.74` prot/s on M1 CPU; `*published*` / `*derived*` in `05_external_modern.md:293-295`). Now embedded directly inside **Foldseek v10+** (`foldseek createdb --prostt5-model`), eliminating PyTorch dependencies (`domainator/docs/esm_3b_foldseek.md:14-24`).
- **Domainator `ESM-2 3B 3Di` (Johnson et al. 2025, *NAR* `gkae1175`, `PMC11754643` + `docs/esm_3b_foldseek.md`):** `ESM-2 3B` + CNN head (`esmologs`, `Zenodo 8174960`), `~1,000×` faster than AlphaFold2, supports proteins up to `2,500` aa (standard AAs only). In Domainator Case 3 (`PMC11754643`), `ESM-2 3B 3Di` + Foldseek searched `124,853` orphan-MTase `±4`-ORF neighborhoods across `366 GB` of GenBank genomes → `2,628` cryptic RM contigs → validated novel Type II REase `Gba686I`. On `FeSOD_20`, ProstT5 recovers the exact same `400/400` hits as `ESM-2 3B 3Di` with `~10%` lower bitscores (`esm_3b_foldseek.md:32-33`).
- **Why `3Di` dominates multi-domain proteins:** Because `3Di` is a **per-residue** 20-letter alphabet (`1 B/aa` raw, `~0.4 B/aa` zstd, `~120 B/ORF`), Foldseek/reseek runs local Gotoh–Smith–Waterman alignment on sub-regions — completely immune to multi-domain pooling dilution and fragmented partial ORFs.

### 2.3 Paradigm (c): Dense-Embedding Remote Homology & Per-Residue Soft Alignment

- **Global Pooled Vector ANN (`TM-Vec`, `PLMSearch`, `ESM-C`):**
  - **`TM-Vec` (Hamamsy et al. 2023, *Nat Biotechnol*, `PMC11180608`):** Twin neural network over frozen `ProtT5-XL-UniRef50` (`1,024`-dim) trained to predict TM-score in a **`512`-dim** cosine space indexed by **FAISS IVF**, paired with `DeepBLAST` for alignment. Encodes `50,000` queries in `40 min` on 1 GPU (`~21` prot/s, `*derived*`); searches `50,000` queries vs `5M` proteins in **`20 s`** on 1 GPU (`*published*`).
  - **`PLMSearch` (Liu et al. 2024, *Nat Commun*, `PMC10981738`):** `ESM-1b` (`1,280`-dim) + PfamClan prefilter + `SS-predictor` (TM-score MLP). Recovers **`1,087 / 1,105`** remote Swiss-Prot pairs (vs Foldseek `934`, MMseqs2 `183`), SCOPe40-test superfamily AUROC **`0.826`** (vs MMseqs2 `0.050`), running CPU-only in `~1.1 min`/query vs UniRef50 (`53.6M`, 64-core Xeon, 512 GB RAM; `05_external_modern.md:305`). **Caveat:** Depends on PfamClan prefiltering for speed (blind to Pfam-dark proteins unless falling back to full scan) and global pooling dilutes multi-domain fusions.
  - **`ESM-C` (EvolutionaryScale 2024/2025):** Modern encoder-only pLM (`300M` / `960`-dim, `600M` / `1,152`-dim, `6B` / `2,560`-dim) trained on `~2.8B` proteins with RoPE and native **FlashAttention2** (`bf16`), context `2,048` aa — `2–4×` higher throughput per parameter than legacy `ESM2` (`*estimate*` from RoPE + FlashAttention2 + no bias).
- **Per-Residue Embedding Local Alignment (`pLM-BLAST`, `EBA`, `DEDAL`):**
  - **`pLM-BLAST` (Kaminski et al. 2023, *Bioinformatics* `btad579`, `PMC10576641`):** Local Smith–Waterman on cosine-similarity matrices of per-residue `ProtT5` (`1,024`-dim) embeddings (`30`-aa convolution window). Matches **HHsearch** accuracy on ECOD `<30%` ID ("hard", `1,500` domains / `300` H-groups) without MSAs, and handles multi-domain fusions natively. **Fatal corpus-scale bottleneck:** storing per-residue `fp16` embeddings (`324 aa × 1,024 × 2 B = 663 KB/ORF`, `*derived*`) would require **`6.16 TB` just for `r26.11.1`'s `9.29M` `NR50` reps**! Therefore viable **only** as an on-demand Round-only tool (Option D) over `10^2–10^4` candidates.
  - **`EBA` (Pantolini et al. 2024, *Bioinformatics* `btad786`, `PMC10792726`):** Signal-enhanced (z-normalized) per-residue `ProtT5`/`ESM-1b` embedding matrix + local/global DP alignment (`~0.02 s/pair` on CPU with precomputed embeddings; `~0.02 s/seq` embedding on GPU). Same storage constraint as `pLM-BLAST` — ideal for candidate-scale refinement, impossible corpus-wide.

## Section 3. `bio-box` Architecture, Resource Sizing & Gains Analysis

### 3.1 Hard Constraints from `bio-box` Repo Specs (`AGENTS.md` §3–§7)

Any pLM/gLM extension MUST obey all existing invariants:
- **Content-addressed keys (`AGENTS.md` §3.2–§3.4):** Features keyed by `feature_uid`, proteins by `protein_uid` (`FIXED_LEN_BYTE_ARRAY(16)`), never by tape `ordinal`. Release-local `token_id`s stay inside `release=` partitions.
- **No materialized pairs (`AGENTS.md` §3.5):** Never materialize `slot_pairs`; operon/neighborhood context is read dynamically from `contig_tapes` (`l3/tapes`) + `tape_tokens` + `protein_occurrence` (`dist_to_contig_end_bp`).
- **Immutability & partition scope (`AGENTS.md` §3.7–§3.10):** Every table is a registered `TableSpec` in `src/biobox/registry/schemas.py`, sorted by `sort_key` for `O(1)` row-group pruning (`AGENTS.md` §4.4). `l0seq` and prior stages are never rewritten; on-demand inference uses the `round=<id>` pattern (`round_domains`, `round_coverage`, `C-38e/f/g`).
- **Pod & Storage Envelope (`AGENTS.md` §4.1, §7.2–§7.3; `STATUS.md:213-224`):** `wilkcomp02` has **zero GPUs**, `40` daytime / `56` nighttime CPU threads, `200 GB` `MemoryMax`, `300 CPU-min` per process, `~940–1,100 GB` volatile `/tmp` SSD (`533 GB` free on 2026-11-19), and `20 TB` free on permanent `/stor/work/Wilke` with a **`>= 64 MiB` file floor** (`LY-04`). GPU compute runs on `wilkcomp03` (local arrangement) or **TACC Lonestar6 (`3× A100-40GB/node`, `C-37`) / TACC Vista (`GH200 96GB`)**.

### 3.2 Four Architectural Options Compared

#### Option A: `SD-21` 1D Structural-Alphabet Pack (`3Di` on `NR50` Representatives)
- **Design:** Predict `3Di` strings (`1 B/aa` raw, `~0.4 B/aa` zstd, `~120 B/ORF` at `324` aa) for every `NR50` representative protein (`protein_uid`-keyed Parquet table `l1_3di` partitioned by `(encoder_version, stage)` or release search-pack shard `3di_nr50/`), using the winner of the `SD-21` bake-off (`ProstT5` via Foldseek v10 vs `ESM-2 3B 3Di`). Searched locally on `wilkcomp02` CPU via `Foldseek` (`--sort-by-structure-bits 0` uses `~0.65 kB/entry`, holding `~3.1e8` reps in `200 GB` RAM, `05_external_modern.md:288`) or `reseek`, then joined to `contig_tapes` via `bio-box nbr`.
- **Strengths:** Solves multi-domain fusions and fragmented `1.5–5 kb` contigs natively (local Smith–Waterman alignment); `10–20×` smaller on disk than dense vectors; zero PyTorch runtime at query time; deterministic and content-addressed by `(encoder_version, protein_uid)`.
- **Weaknesses:** Requires a query seed (sequence or structure) — cannot score a wholly unannotated *de novo* defense operon with no known structural homolog.

#### Option B: Corpus-Wide Dense Vector / ANN Pack (`(encoder_version, protein_uid)` on `NR50` or `NR90`)
- **Design:** Precompute pooled `ESM-C 300M` (`960`-dim) or `ESM2-150M` (`640`-dim) vectors for `NR50` (or `NR90`) representatives, stored in `>= 64 MiB` Parquet shards sorted by `protein_uid` (`fp16` = `1.92 KB/ORF` for `960`d; `int8` scalar-quantized = `960 B/ORF`), plus a release-scoped **FAISS `IVF-PQ`** (`64–128 B/ORF`) or **`RaBitQ` 4-bit/1-bit** (`120–480 B/ORF`) ANN index for sub-second `wilkcomp02` queries inside `200 GB` RAM.
- **Solving Multi-Domain Dilution:** Whole-ORF mean pooling fails when a `200`-aa novel nuclease is fused to a `1,000`-aa helicase. Option B **must** embed `segments.faa` (`SEARCH_PLAN.md` `SD-06`, Pattern H: Pfam-uncovered regions `>= 50` aa + excised domain envelopes, `~1.5×` ORF count) or sliding `256`-aa overlapping windows (`stride 128`), which increases storage and GPU cost by `~1.5–2.0×` (`*estimate*`).
- **Verdict:** Economical at `r26.11.1` (`18 GB` `fp16` for `9.29M` `NR50`), borderline at `0.92 Tbp` (`~192 GB` `fp16`, `~12 GB` `IVF-PQ`), and **prohibitively expensive + redundant with `3Di`** at `14.6 Tbp` (`2.4 TB` `fp16` raw, `~150–300 GB` ANN index).

#### Option C: Tape-Context Operon / Defense-Island Scorer (over `contig_tapes`)
- **Design:** A lightweight contextual head — either a `GeneCLR_DF`-style `12`-layer Double-Stranded Attention Transformer (`480`d) or a `DefensePredictor`-style LightGBM — scanning `contig_tapes` (`±10` slots, `delta_bp_prev_slots`, `junction_topology_prevs`, `strands`), looking up precomputed `NR90` (or `NR50`) rep vectors via `member_cluster` + `O(1)` pruned vector shards, and fusing `bio-box`'s existing **CRISPR arrays (`l1_crispr`)**, **Pfam tokens (`domain_family_tokens`)**, and **MGE/plasmid/phage labels (`genome_dim.kind`, `PL1`/`PL2`/`V1`/`H1`)**.
- **Edge-Censoring Fix for Short Contigs:** Replaces `DefensePredictor`'s broken genome-wide GC z-scoring and sentinel filling (`0/1.1/2/-200`) with explicit attention masking + `dist_to_contig_end_bp` (`protein_occurrence`) and `orfs_to_contig_starts`/`orfs_to_contig_ends` (`contig_tapes`), and computes GC anomaly relative to the **contig's own genus/species median** (from `contig_dim`) or omits it on `<10 kb` unbinned contigs.
- **Quantifying `NR50` vs `NR90` Representative Distortion:** Substituting an `NR50` rep vector (`50%` AA identity) for the exact `protein_uid` introduces substantial cosine drift (`0.05–0.15` cosine distance in ESM2 space, `*estimate*`) and can swap a defensive paralog with a non-defensive housekeeping homolog inside the same 50%-ID cluster (the exact failure mode `DefensePredictor` was built to overcome!). `NR90` reps (`>= 90%` ID, `80%` cov) preserve `>0.98` cosine similarity (`*estimate*`) at `3.56×` the count of `NR50` in `r26.11.1` (`33.12M / 9.29M`, `*derived*`). Therefore, Option C **must** use `NR90` (or exact `protein_uid` inside candidate rounds), never `NR50` reps.

#### Option D: On-Demand Round-Only GPU Inference (`C-38e/f/g` Content-Addressed Pattern)
- **Design:** Zero corpus-wide dense embeddings. Register a content-addressed `round=<id>` GPU step (`bio-box annotate plm-round` / `search plm-align`) that runs `ESM-C 300M/600M`, `DefensePredictor`/`GeneCLR_DF`, or per-residue **`pLM-BLAST`/`EBA`** **only** on targeted subsets:
  1. `±10`-gene neighborhoods around `bio-box search hmm` / `Foldseek-3Di` seed hits (`10^3–10^5` proteins);
  2. Recurrent unannotated `NR50` cluster reps above size `K` (e.g., `size >= 5` or MGE/CRISPR-enriched, `DEC-54 Q14`);
  3. **Filtered `dark_orf`s only:** In `r26.11.1`, there are **`123,441,968` `dark_orf`s** (`2.51×` all `49.10M` distinct called proteins, `*measured*`, `2026-11-16_release_r26.11.1.md:230`). Bulk-embedding raw `dark_orf`s is a massive compute/FP trap because `>95%` are spurious stop-to-stop translations in non-coding frames and `3 kb` contig-end zones (`C-38` §2.3). Instead, pre-filter `dark_orf`s to those within `±5` genes of a CRISPR array / MGE anchor OR clustered across `>= 3` distinct taxonomic orders (`PLAN.md` smORF rule), shrinking `123.44M` to `<10^5` candidates (`>1,000×` reduction, `*estimate*`).

### 4.1 Held-Out Benchmark Protocol on `r26.11.1`

1. **Positive Set (Strict Homology Holdout):**
   - Map all curated `DefenseFinder` (`>= 152` systems), Millman 2022, and Laub's `42` experimentally validated `DS-1..DS-42` systems (`PMC13092281` Table S7) onto `r26.11.1` (`S1, P1–P6, H1, PL1`) by exact/high-identity sequence match (`>= 90%` ID, `>= 80%` cov).
   - **Strict Holdout Filter (closing DefensePredictor's `30%` gap):** Partition families so that **every held-out test protein** has **`<20%` AA identity** against the entire query/training split under `mmseqs search -s 7.5` (3-iter profile) **AND** **`hmmsearch E > 10`** (at fixed `Z_prot = 1e8`, `SD-04`) against all training-split HMMs. Report performance separately on: (a) `<20%` ID & `E > 10` remote homologs, and (b) completely novel-fold systems (`Foldseek E > 1` against training structures).
2. **Three Mandatory Hard Stratifications:**
   - **Stratification (i) — Multi-domain fusion proteins:** Held-out defense effectors fused to `>= 1` non-defense or promiscuous partner domain (e.g., `NACHT+effector`, `TniQ+nuclease`, `helicase+PD-(D/E)XK`, length `>= 600` aa, including `>1,022` aa proteins to expose `ESM2` truncation).
   - **Stratification (ii) — Closed vs Fragmented Contigs:** Take the **exact same** held-out operons on closed `P1–P6` (`S2C`) chromosomes and run an **artificial contig-truncation ablation** slicing each locus into `1.5–5 kb` fragments drawn from `SPIRE`'s empirical contig-length distribution (`C-42`), measuring recall/precision drop when `±2` / `±10` neighbors are censored and genome-wide GC is unavailable.
   - **Stratification (iii) — Real Background False-Positive Traps:** Score against a stratified sample of `1,000,000` real `r26.11.1` non-defense genes (including enriched trap cohorts of **housekeeping P-loop NTPases/helicases, Rossmann methyltransferases, solitary IS/transposases, and metabolic hydrolases** drawn from `r26.11.1`'s `57.09M` genes) to measure true genome-wide FPR, not balanced-dataset AUROC.
3. **Competitors Evaluated Head-to-Head on Identical Splits:**
   - **Baselines (CPU, already in `bio-box`):** `3-round jackhmmer` (`Z = 1e8`), `HHblits` (3-iter vs Pfam/PDB70), `bio-box nbr` guilt-by-association (`F-01`/`F-03`).
   - **Option A (`SD-21`):** `ProstT5` (Foldseek v10) vs `ESM-2 3B 3Di` (Domainator) + `Foldseek` / `reseek`.
   - **Option B:** `ESM-C 300M` pooled (`fp16` vs `IVF-PQ 64B`), full-ORF vs `segments.faa` uncovered-region pooling.
